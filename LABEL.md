# It Sees You, It Names You

Ford Wong and Ethan Takher

Arduino Nano, HC-SR04 ultrasonic distance sensor, HC-SR501 PIR motion sensor,
SSD1306 OLED screen, breadboard, 5V wall charger

2026

It notices how near you are, in centimetres, and whether you are moving. It
calls you hoverer, passerby, waiting or nobody. Then it says that name on its
screen, puts the number it judged you on right underneath, and blinks that
name's own pattern on its light. It refuses to count your visits, to save a
single reading, or to remember you at all once it is unplugged.
