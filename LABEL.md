Too Close
Fordham Wong and Ethan Takher
Arduino Nano, HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor, PIR motion sensor, OLED screen, cardboard, 2026

It notices how close you stand and whether you move. It calls you hoverer, passerby or nobody. Then it shows your distance and speed on a small screen, and fades a bigger screen from black to white and says NEAR as you lean in. It refuses to remember how long you stood there.
